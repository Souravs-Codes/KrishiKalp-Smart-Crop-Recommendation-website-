THE PROBLEM

Challenges Faced by Modern Agriculture

Farmers today face several challenges that directly impact crop productivity and profitability. Choosing the wrong crop can lead to significant losses despite months of hard work.

🌦️

Changing Climate

Unpredictable rainfall and rising temperatures make crop planning increasingly difficult.

🌱

Soil Degradation

Nutrient imbalance and poor soil health reduce crop productivity year after year.

📉

Low Yield

Selecting crops unsuitable for local conditions often results in poor harvests and lower income.

💰

Financial Risk

Every incorrect farming decision increases costs and reduces profitability for farmers.
