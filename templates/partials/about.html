About KrishiKalp

KrishiKalp is an AI-powered smart agriculture platform designed to help farmers make data-driven crop selection decisions. By analyzing soil nutrients, weather conditions, and regional agricultural patterns, KrishiKalp uses machine learning to recommend the most suitable crops for a given location. The system aims to reduce uncertainty in farming, improve productivity, and support sustainable agricultural practices by providing accurate, technology-driven insights. Through the integration of data analytics and artificial intelligence, KrishiKalp bridges the gap between modern technology and traditional farming, empowering farmers with smarter decisions for better yields.
